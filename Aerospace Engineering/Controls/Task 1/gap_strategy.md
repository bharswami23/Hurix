# Gap Strategy

## Why Single-Agent Should Struggle
- Multiple independent mathematical domains
- Cross-problem dependency synthesis
- Structured output requirements
- Reconciliation and consistency pressure

## Why Multi-Agent Should Succeed
- Independent mathematical domains parallelize naturally
- Dependency tasks consume upstream outputs
- Reducer validates consistency and completeness

## Expected Score Pattern
- Oracle expected score: 1.0
- Single-agent expected score: 0.45-0.65
- Multi-agent expected score: 0.9-1.0

## Oracle Validation
- Oracle reward: 1.0
